# Privacy

PoE Personal Trade does not operate an account system and does not collect analytics, telemetry, passwords, session cookies, or Path of Exile credentials.

When the user triggers a price check, the copied item text is parsed locally. Search filters are sent directly from the app to the official Path of Exile Trade service. Normal network metadata such as an IP address is therefore visible to Grinding Gear Games under its own policies.

The app stores the selected language and league in a local `settings.json` file inside Electron's application-data directory. If you explicitly teach the app a modifier mapping, that confirmed mapping is saved locally in `learned-modifiers.json`; it is not uploaded. Uninstalling the app may leave these small files behind; they can be deleted safely.
